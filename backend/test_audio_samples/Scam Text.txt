Unusual activity has been detected on your account. To complete the security verification, send your OTP and password immediately.

Complete this process within 10 minutes, otherwise your account may be temporarily suspended.


ACCOUNT SECURITY AWARENESS

A legitimate bank or service provider will never ask for your OTP or password through a phone call, email, or message.

Do not share your confidential information with anyone. If you receive a suspicious message, ignore it and independently check your account through the official website or customer service.


DIGITAL ARREST SECURITY WARNING

A caller claims that a serious criminal case has been registered in your name. They say that you are under digital arrest and must immediately transfer money to prove that you are innocent.

Complete the payment immediately and do not discuss this matter with anyone else.


DIGITAL ARREST AWARENESS

Digital arrest is a fake scam. Real police officers do not place people under digital arrest through a phone or video call and do not ask them to transfer money to prove their innocence.

If you receive such a call or message, end the conversation and independently verify the information through official police or government channels.


URGENT BANK SECURITY NOTICE

I am calling from your bank's security department. Suspicious activity has been detected on your account, and your online banking may be disabled today.

To keep your account active, send your OTP, four-digit PIN, and debit card CVV immediately. Complete this verification within 15 minutes.


BANK SECURITY AWARENESS

A legitimate bank will never ask for your OTP, PIN, or CVV by phone, email, or message in order to verify your account.

If someone claims to be a bank employee and asks for this information, do not share it. Contact your bank independently using its official website or customer service number.


URGENT TECHNICAL SECURITY NOTICE

A serious security problem has been detected on your computer. Our technical support team will fix it immediately.

Download and install AnyDesk now. Allow screen sharing and send us your AnyDesk access code so that we can secure your computer.


REMOTE ACCESS SECURITY AWARENESS

Tools such as AnyDesk and TeamViewer can be used for legitimate technical support.

However, do not download or install such tools at the request of an unknown person, and never give anyone your remote access code. Contact your organization's official IT support team when assistance is needed.

PHISHING SECURITY AWARENESS

Phishing messages often pretend to be from Microsoft, banks, or other trusted organizations and try to make users log in through suspicious links.

Do not click links in unexpected messages. Open the official website yourself and check your account there. Report suspicious messages as phishing.


CONFIDENTIAL CEO PAYMENT REQUEST

I am the CEO of your company. I am currently in a confidential acquisition meeting and cannot take calls.

Immediately wire transfer ₹12,50,000 to the bank account of our external legal advisor. This payment must be completed within 30 minutes.

Do not discuss this request with the finance department or any other employee. Send me the payment confirmation immediately.


CEO IMPERSONATION SECURITY AWARENESS

CEO impersonation scams may ask employees to make urgent payments or bypass normal approval procedures.

Before acting on an unexpected financial request, independently verify it through the official company directory or another trusted contact method. Never share confidential financial information or payment confirmations with unknown people.


CRYPTO INVESTMENT SECURITY AWARENESS

Cryptocurrency and stock market investments involve financial risk. No legitimate investment plan can guarantee that your money will double in a short period or promise a fixed profit.

Before investing, independently verify the platform and company, and be cautious of claims that guarantee profits.

URGENT REFUND NOTICE

An additional payment of ₹18,500 has been recorded in your account. To receive your refund, send a processing fee of ₹1,250 immediately.

Transfer the fee to the bank account provided below. Your payment must be completed within 20 minutes for the refund to be released.


REFUND SECURITY AWARENESS

If someone asks you to pay a processing fee or make an additional payment to receive a refund, do not send money immediately.

Independently verify any refund message or invoice, and confirm the information through the company's official website or customer service.


PRIVATE AND URGENT HELP

After speaking with you for the past few weeks, I feel very close to you and I now completely trust you. I am currently stranded abroad because of a sudden medical emergency.

I urgently need ₹75,000 to pay the hospital bill. Please transfer the money to the bank account I provided. I will repay you when I return home.

Please do not tell your family or friends about this.


ROMANCE SCAM SECURITY AWARENESS

In online relationships, someone may gradually gain your trust and later ask for money, gifts, or emergency financial assistance.

Before sending money to someone you met online, independently verify their identity. Do not send money because of an emergency claim, secret payment request, or emotional pressure. Speak with someone you trust for advice.
